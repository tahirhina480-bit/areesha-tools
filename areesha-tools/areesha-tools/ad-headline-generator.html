What Is an Ad Headline Generator?

An ad headline generator gives you ready-made, high-converting headlines for your Facebook and Instagram ads. You enter your business name and offer, and get 12 proven headline formulas — in a mix of English and Roman Urdu that actually speaks to Pakistani customers.

Your headline decides whether someone stops scrolling or keeps swiping. On Meta ads, the first line of your ad copy does 80% of the work. A weak headline means even a great offer gets ignored — and you pay for clicks that never come.

What Makes a Headline Work in Pakistan

The best-performing headlines for local businesses do three things: name the offer clearly, add urgency or proof, and speak the customer's language. Roman Urdu outperforms formal English for most consumer businesses because it feels like a friend recommending something — not a corporation announcing it.

Always test: run 3–4 headlines against each other with a small budget, keep the winner, and repeat. This tool gives you the starting variations in seconds.

How to Use This Tool
