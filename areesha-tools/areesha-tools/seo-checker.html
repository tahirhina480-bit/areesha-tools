What Is a Website SEO Checker?

A website SEO checker is a free online tool that evaluates how well your website is prepared to appear in Google search results. SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is simply the practice of making your website easy for Google to understand and attractive for customers to click. When someone in Lahore searches "best dental clinic near me" or "cake shop in DHA", Google decides which businesses to show — and good SEO is what puts you on that list.

Our free checker automatically analyzes the ten things that matter most for Pakistani small businesses: HTTPS security, your page title, meta description, mobile-friendliness, main heading, image descriptions, WhatsApp button, visible contact info, social media links, and loading speed. You get a score from 0 to 100 plus a prioritized list of exactly what to fix first.

Why SEO Matters for Pakistani Small Businesses

Most customers today search on Google before they visit or call. If your competitor appears in the search results and you don't, they get the customer — it's that simple. The good news: local SEO in Pakistan is far less competitive than in Western markets. A clinic or shop that completes the basics — a proper Google Business Profile, correct contact details, a mobile-friendly site, and a steady stream of reviews — can often outrank bigger competitors within a few months.

SEO is also the cheapest marketing channel you have. Unlike ads, which stop the moment you stop paying, a well-optimized website and Google profile keep bringing customers month after month, for free.

How to Improve Your Score (The Big Wins)

  1. Claim your Google Business Profile. It's free, takes 20 minutes, and is the single biggest factor for appearing on Google Maps.
  2. Collect reviews relentlessly. Ask every happy customer. Businesses with 20+ recent reviews dramatically outperform those with none.
  3. Make your phone number impossible to miss — in the header, footer, and contact page — and add a WhatsApp chat link so customers can reach you in one click.
  4. Write clear page titles that include your service and city, e.g. "Best Biryani in Gulberg, Lahore | Al-Madina Foods".
  5. Keep things fresh. Add new photos, offers, or a short post every few weeks so Google sees an active business.
