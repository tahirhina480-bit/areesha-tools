What Is a Google Review Link Generator?

A Google review link generator turns your business's Google listing into a single link that opens the review form directly. Instead of telling customers "Google par review de dein" and hoping they find you, you send one tap-able link — or a QR code they can scan at your counter.

For clinics, salons, restaurants and shops in Pakistan, Google reviews are pure gold. They decide whether a stranger on Google Maps picks you or your competitor. Businesses with 20+ recent reviews dramatically outperform those with none — and every review also helps your Google Maps ranking.

Why a Direct Link Gets You 5x More Reviews

Customers are happy to review you — but only if it takes ten seconds. Every extra step (search your name, find the listing, scroll to reviews, tap "write a review") loses people. A direct link removes all friction: tap, stars, done. Print the QR code on your bills, visiting cards and counter stand, and ask every happy customer on their way out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this review link generator free?

Yes — 3 free generations per day. Pro members (Rs. 500/month) get unlimited use.

Where do I find my Place ID?

Use Google's official Place ID finder (linked above): type your business name, click your listing, and copy the Place ID shown.

Will the link work if my business isn't on Google Maps?

No — your business must have a Google Business Profile first. That's free to set up and takes about 20 minutes.

Can I put the QR code on my flyers?

Absolutely — that's the best use. Counter stands, bills, visiting cards and flyers all work great.
