What Is a UTM Link Builder?

A UTM link builder adds small tracking tags to your URLs so Google Analytics can tell you exactly which ad, post or message brought each visitor. Instead of guessing whether your Facebook ad or your WhatsApp broadcast worked, you see it in black and white.

Example: you run the same offer on Facebook and Instagram. With UTM tags, your analytics shows "40 visitors from facebook / cpc / eid-sale" vs "12 from instagram" — now you know where to put your next rupee. Without tags, it's all one blurry "direct traffic" number.

The 3 Tags That Matter

Source (where: facebook, whatsapp, google), medium (how: cpc, post, broadcast) and campaign (which offer: eid-sale, new-branch). Term and content are optional extras for paid keywords and ad variations. Keep names lowercase, short and consistent — "Facebook", "facebook" and "FB" will show as three different sources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this UTM builder free?

Yes — 3 free links per day. Pro members (Rs. 500/month) get unlimited use.

Do I need Google Analytics for this to work?

To see the reports, yes — install free Google Analytics on your website. The tags themselves work regardless.

Will the long URL look bad in my ad?

Your ad platform shows its own display text; the full tagged URL works silently in the background.

What naming convention should I follow?

Lowercase, no spaces (use dashes): e.g. source=facebook, medium=cpc, campaign=eid-sale. Be consistent everywhere.
