What Is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces a photo's file size without visibly hurting its quality. A 4 MB phone photo can usually shrink to under 300 KB — which means your website loads faster, your WhatsApp catalog sends quicker, and your Facebook ads get approved without "image too large" errors.

Heavy images are the #1 reason Pakistani small-business websites load slowly. And slow sites lose customers: most visitors leave if a page takes more than 3 seconds. Compressing your photos before uploading is the single easiest speed win there is.

Completely Private — Nothing Is Uploaded

Unlike most online compressors, this tool works 100% in your browser. Your photos never leave your device and are never sent to any server. Safe for client photos, CNIC scans and anything sensitive.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Choose a photo from your device.
  2. Set quality (70% is the sweet spot) and max width.
  3. Compress, compare the sizes, and download the smaller file.
